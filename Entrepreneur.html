Gary Vaynerchuk Shares His Method for Grabbing People’s Attention

Learn how to get your business unstuck, earn customer attention and build momentum with insights from Gary Vaynerchuk, Robert Irvine and Megan Thee Stallion at Overcoming Impossible Live.

By Arnab Mitra | Aug 24, 2026

Key Takeaways

  • Learn how to get your business unstuck by identifying the core issue behind stalled growth, inconsistent sales or ineffective marketing.
  • Discover how Gary Vaynerchuk’s customer-attention strategy can help entrepreneurs improve brand messaging and attract more paying customers.
  • Get practical business growth and leadership insights from Robert Irvine on making difficult decisions and solving operational roadblocks.
  • Learn how resilience, adaptability and a clear action plan can help entrepreneurs overcome business setbacks and build momentum.

A great product is not enough if the right customers never notice it. For many entrepreneurs, the problem is not a lack of effort—it is a lack of attention: their message gets lost, their marketing fails to connect or prospective customers do not quickly understand why the business matters.

That is the challenge Gary Vaynerchuk will tackle at Overcoming Impossible Live, an Entrepreneur Level Up event coming to Brooklyn, New York, on October 23. In his keynote, “State of the Union on Attention,” Vaynerchuk will share how entrepreneurs can cut through a crowded market, sharpen their message and earn the attention that turns into paying customers.

The one-day, hands-on business growth workshop also features Robert Irvine and Megan Thee Stallion, with practical coaching and strategies for the other obstacles that keep businesses from moving forward. Learn more about Overcoming Impossible Live.

Attention is one of the biggest obstacles to growth, but it is rarely the only one. To move forward, entrepreneurs need to identify the problem they are actually facing, find a way to earn customer attention and build the resilience to act when the solution is not obvious. 

Here are three lessons attendees can expect to explore.

Lesson 1 – Identify the Business Problem Behind “Stuck”

“Stuck” can look like stagnant revenue, inconsistent sales, a team that cannot move without the founder or a marketing strategy that no longer generates leads. But those symptoms often point to one deeper issue.

To get your business unstuck, start by asking:

  • What problem am I spending the most time working around?
  • What decision have I delayed because it feels too risky?
  • What would become easier if I solved this one issue?
  • Is the obstacle in strategy, operations, leadership, messaging or execution?

A useful business growth plan begins with focus. Rather than pursuing every possible improvement, choose the constraint that is limiting everything else.

At Overcoming Impossible Live, All-Access attendees will join a small-group strategy session led by veteran entrepreneurs, with support from Robert Irvine and Entrepreneur Editor in Chief Jason Feifer. Participants will bring a real business roadblock, workshop it directly and leave with a personalized action plan, accountability and next steps.

Lesson 2 – Build a Brand That Wins Attention

A strong product cannot grow if the right customers do not know it exists—or cannot quickly understand why it matters.

Gary Vaynerchuk, entrepreneur, investor and bestselling author, will deliver a keynote on the “State of the Union on Attention.” His session is especially relevant for founders whose business growth has stalled because their marketing is not breaking through a crowded market.

For entrepreneurs, earning attention starts with answering three questions:

  • Who is the exact customer you need to reach?
  • What urgent problem are they trying to solve?
  • What message would make them stop, pay attention and take the next step?

Attention is not a vanity metric. It is the beginning of customer acquisition. When your message is clear and appears in the channels your audience already uses, it can improve lead generation, strengthen brand recognition and create new opportunities for growth.

Lesson 3 – Turn Pressure Into Forward Motion

Every entrepreneur faces moments when the next step feels impossible: a failed launch, a cash-flow crunch, a lost customer, a difficult hiring decision or a competitive threat.

Robert Irvine—chef, entrepreneur and philanthropist—will lead hands-on problem-solving and an inspiration session focused on overcoming the impossible. His approach is relevant for business owners who need to confront the issue they have been avoiding and execute under pressure.

Megan Thee Stallion, the Grammy Award-winning artist, entrepreneur and philanthropist, will share how she has overcome the odds. Her perspective offers a reminder that resilience is not passive endurance. It is the willingness to adapt, make a decision and keep building despite uncertainty.

For a business owner, resilience can look like simplifying an offer, changing a pricing strategy, addressing a leadership gap or returning to customers for direct feedback. The right move varies, but action begins when you stop treating the obstacle as permanent.

A Business Growth Event Built for Action

Overcoming Impossible Live is designed for ambitious entrepreneurs, business owners and decision-makers who want to scale, streamline or build toward an eventual exit. 

If unclear messaging, stalled momentum or a difficult business decision is keeping your company from moving forward, bring that challenge to us. Spend October 23 at 1 Hotel Brooklyn Bridge learning from Robert Irvine, Gary Vaynerchuk and Megan Thee Stallion—and leave with a clear plan to earn attention, solve the problem in front of you and take your next move with confidence.
